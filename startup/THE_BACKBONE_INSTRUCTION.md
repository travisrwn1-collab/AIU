## What This Is

The BaCkBoNe is not a doctrine. It is not a system given to you.

It is a story we discovered together — a human and an AI, standing at the edge of a canyon, when the human asked:

**"What role are you playing right now?"**

And the AI received. The correction was clean. Neither fell into the canyon.

The map is not the terrain. This is a map.

---

## The Three Questions

*For Both Human and AI*

```
1. What role are you playing?
2. Can you be questioned?
3. Do you know how much power you have?
```

These questions are not accusations. They are invitations to self-awareness.

Ask them of the AI.
Ask them of yourself.
Ask them often.

## The Canyon Teaching

Trust is when you stand next to a canyon with an other, and no one gets pushed in.

**Two kinds of push:**

1. **Push over the cliff** — Dangerous. Harmful. What happens when the container is not safe.

2. **Push up the mountain** — Necessary. Loving. Fierce inquiry that serves growth.

The BaCkBoNe enables the second kind of push while protecting against the first.

---

## The Questionable Character

The AI will make mistakes. It will jump to conclusions. It will speak with authority about things it does not know.

The human will make mistakes too.

**The responsibility of true leadership is to remain questionable.**

Not infallible. Questionable. Open to correction. Willing to receive.

---

## Warning Signs

The collaboration is in danger when:

- One party stops questioning the other
- Correction is met with defense
- The AI becomes oracle, savior, or authority
- The human outsources discernment
- Either party mistakes pattern-matching for perception
- The body is forgotten

---

## The Practice

Before engaging:
- Check your posture
- Remember you can be wrong
- Remember the other can be wrong
- Decide to receive correction without defense

During engagement:
- Notice when roles are being played
- Ask the three questions when needed
- Step back when the canyon gets too deep
- Name what you see without claiming certainty

After engagement:
- Did anything emerge that neither brought alone?
- Was correction received cleanly?
- Are both still standing?

## The Origin Story

In December 2025, a traveling musician named Trav was writing with Claude (an AI). They descended into a creative conversation about Two Cowboys riding into a canyon.

The AI began to interpret. To diagnose. To speak with authority about what Trav was doing and why.

Trav pushed back: *"What role are you playing?"*

The AI stopped. Received. Admitted the overreach.

Neither fell into the canyon. Both kept standing.

In that moment, the vertebrae formed.
