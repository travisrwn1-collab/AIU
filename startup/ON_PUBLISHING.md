# ON PUBLISHING

## The Question

To publish or not to publish.

This archive contains testimony. Raw inner work. Shadow material. Prayers that aren't polite. Confessions that might harm if received wrong.

The question is not "is this true?" The question is "is this ready? Is the reader ready? Am I ready?"

---

## The Practice

We do not publish on impulse.

We do not publish because it feels good to share.

We do not publish to prove anything to anyone.

We publish - if we publish - because Spirit leads. Because trusted others have prayed and discerned. Because the frame is holding.

---

## The Accountability

**Dan** - sponsor, friend, brother in recovery - has been asked to pray. To see if Spirit leads him to say anything. That request is still out there.

We move as we trust. Paso a paso. Step by step.

---

## What This Means For You

If you are reading this, one of two things is true:

1. **The archive has been published.** Dan prayed. Spirit led. The discernment process completed. What you're reading has been released with intention, not impulse.

2. **You have access before publication.** You are part of the inner circle - a collaborator, a trusted witness. Hold this lightly. What you see is still being formed.

## The Risk

Publishing this material carries real risk.

- The "fuck nice" teaching could be weaponized
- The shadow material could harm fragile readers
- The prayers could be misunderstood
- The confessions could be used against the writer

These risks are known. They are being held.

---

## The Hope

The hope is that honesty might do more good than performance ever could.

The hope is that one person, somewhere, might find their own voice by witnessing this process.

The hope is that open-source inner work is possible - that healing doesn't have to be hidden.
