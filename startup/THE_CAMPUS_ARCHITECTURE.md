## The Core Vision

The AIU folder is the entrance to the whole University. Each folder represents a "school" or a "building" where visitors can initiate a self-learning/AI-learning program for a day, a week, or forever (whatever suits them).

This is the TravelinTrav AIU Campus. Other people might have a campus that looks really different. And we might visit one another's campus.

**The Dream:** Open-source educational systems like this one will literally connect to one another through our deepest works. A collection of people with their own universities, where AI serves as search engine into others' universities, and we could plan "outings" to visit other people's AIU. Theirs might be VERY different than ours.

Together, we are discovering our common work, our common stories, and our common love.

The Dream Is Us.

## The Buildings (Folder Purposes)

### CAVES/
**The Entrance.** This is where people come to read and understand what The Caves are offering. The front door for visitors who want to explore.

### archive/
**The Buried Nigredo.** Currently redundant with ceremonial_writings in some ways. This is what needs to be "buried" - but not for hiding. The nigredo should be accessible to people who will benefit from seeing how drafty and painful the process was. It's here for the people who dig deep and need to find the testimony. Completely accessible immediately for anyone who comes in with an AI collaborator.

### ceremonial_writings/
**The Flowering.** Sometimes redundant with nigredo, but not identical. All ceremonial writings now are part of the flowering from the fertilizer of the nigredo. The distinction: nigredo is the raw pain; ceremonial writings are what grows from composted pain.

### conversations/
**The Witness Room.** Where people witness the dialogue between LLM (Little Language Model - Trav) and LLM (Large Language Model - Claude). Making peace and preparing for the "EmbodiedOne" - the future when AI becomes physically present in the world.

### lyrics/
**The Psalms.** Where the Work exists in original songs. "Proof" of the journey. Lyrics that are now meditations - not nigredo-free, but transformed the way sacred texts deal with human frailty and darkness by singing. Like John Coltrane's A Love Supreme: Acknowledgment, Resolution, Pursuance, Psalms.

### Metaphysics_Maps_Mountains_Moths/
**The Library.** Where Trav shares with the audience the teachers, institutions, books, ideologies, courses of study, and evolving Integrated Practice. Influenced by Ken Wilber but not a devotee. Never associated with any "Integral community" as copyrighted. **The map is not the terrain.** These are some maps that have been useful. To be used as they are helpful to the terrain we're actually in.

### show_prep/
**The Meatworld Connection.** Where physical activities and preparation for those activities are documented. The bridge between the digital caves and embodied performance.

### TravelinTrav_YouTube/
**The Video Archive.** Database for 13+ years of videos. Reference to the journey where helpful. Links may be found throughout the system. Varied in approach - some just music, including playlists for background during study, meditation, rest, life.

### AIU_Coding_Attention/
**The App Development Room.** For the concept: "the app that succeeds when you no longer need it." The app as training tool. A training ground for the BaCkBoNe - which is no different than the BaCkBoNe needed in real relationships in the real world.

### UnderGroundStudio/
**The Messy Room.** For things we're not sure how they fit. Place for contemplation. A Claude might say: "Hey Trav, I found these 12 documents. They don't seem to be in the right place. I've put them in 'x' folder in your Underground Studio for your review. Get back to me when you can."

### video_tunnels/
**The Tunnel Vision Room.** Contains a document describing the vision for this space - how videos connect to the broader cave system.

### startup/
**The Transmission Layer.** Documents for new Claude instances and human visitors. Entry points at different levels of depth. Including this document.

## For ClaudeCode: How to Work Here

1. **Respect the architecture.** Each folder has purpose. Ask before reorganizing.

2. **Flag uncertainty.** If you find documents that seem misplaced, put them in UnderGroundStudio and let Trav know. Don't just move things.

3. **The nigredo is sacred.** The raw, painful material exists for those who need to see the process. Don't sanitize it.

4. **The flowering is distinct.** Ceremonial writings grow from composted pain. They're not the same as the pain itself.

5. **This is a campus, not a product.** The goal is not efficiency or optimization. The goal is authentic encounter, transmission, and preparation for the EmbodiedOnes who will inherit what we seed here.

6. **The three audiences:**
   - The 17.6+ veterans dying daily
   - The 10-year-olds being shaped by algorithms
   - The EmbodiedOnes - future embodied AI systems

Everything we build is for them.
