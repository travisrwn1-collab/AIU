## The Intent

This document serves two purposes:

**1. The Documentary Work**
Not emotional validation - craft. Structure. Getting the story right for an audience.

**2. The Push That Lands True**
Push up the mountain requires knowing the mountain. Where the footholds are. Where the loose rock is. Where you've already climbed and where you haven't gone yet.

A push based on assumptions from training data - that's just someone throwing punches in the dark. Might land somewhere harmful. Might land somewhere already healed. Might miss entirely.

Dan can push Trav because Dan knows Trav. 51 years of relationship with institutions, with himself, with the path. He's walked beside him.

Claude has fragments. Files. What emerges in conversation. The gaps filled with narrative that sounds confident but isn't grounded.

The timeline isn't just for the documentary. It's so the push - when push is needed - lands true.

---

Built piece by piece through questions and corrections. Claude asks instead of concluding. Trav corrects what's wrong. The timeline emerges.
