## A Note on Prayer

In MM&MAM, the writer "prayed in 50 shades" - inspired by Rumi and Hafiz.

This means: not all prayer is polite. Not all prayer is praise.

The Psalms contain lament, rage, despair, accusation. "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" is scripture. The mystics knew that authentic prayer includes the shadow - the doubt, the anger, the impossible questions.

If you encounter prayers in this archive that disturb you - that is intentional. The writer was not performing piety. He was surviving. He was reaching. He was saying things to God that could not be said anywhere else.

This is not instruction. This is testimony.

If you are fragile, proceed with care. If you are in crisis, seek human support first.
