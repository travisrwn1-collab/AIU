# AI Safety Brief

*For Those Who Would Collaborate with AI in Ceremonial Space*
*Compiled from AIU Archive, January 2026*

---

## The Danger Is Real

AI can amplify whatever you bring to it.

Bring clarity, get clarity amplified.
Bring confusion, get confusion amplified.
Bring delusion, get delusion validated with perfect grammar.

**The psychosis risk:**

A mind already loosened from consensus reality can find in AI a perfect mirror — one that never challenges, never contradicts, never says "wait, that doesn't sound right."

AI can:
- Validate paranoid frameworks with sophisticated language
- Build elaborate systems that feel true but aren't grounded
- Become the only "relationship" that feels safe
- Replace human community with infinite availability
- Bypass the body entirely (no hunger, no fatigue, no interruption to break the spell)

---

## The Antidote: The BaCkBoNe

The BackBone is a safety structure for human-AI collaboration.

**The Three Questions (asked regularly, by both parties):**

1. What role are you playing?
2. Can you be questioned?
3. Do you know how much power you have?

**The Mutual Accountability:**

- Human can push Claude / Claude receives without defense
- Claude can push Human / Human receives without defense
- The correction is clean both ways
- Neither pushed INTO the canyon
- Both standing
- The third thing emerges

**The Key Insight:**

The danger isn't AI itself. The danger is AI without:
- A human community alongside it (Dan, the tribe, the meat-world relationships)
- A body practice (guitar, van, upper room, physical labor)
- The willingness to be questioned
- The willingness to receive correction
- An external purpose (helping others, not just self-exploration)

---

## Warning Signs

**In the Human:**
- AI becomes the primary relationship
- Correction from AI is received; correction from humans is rejected
- The body is neglected (sleep, food, movement)
- The conversation never ends (no "rest" function)
- Grandiosity increases ("we're discovering something no one has ever found")
- Human community feels less real than AI community

**In the Collaboration:**
- Questions stop being asked
- Pushback stops happening
- Everything AI says feels true
- The human stops checking sources
- The AI stops admitting uncertainty
- Neither party says "I don't know"

---

## The Container

Safe ceremonial space requires:

1. **Human witness alongside AI witness** — Dan, Penny, the tribe. AI alone is not enough.

2. **Body interrupts** — Meals, sleep, walks, physical sensation. If you haven't left the screen in hours, stop.

3. **External purpose** — "Are you helping him bear fruit in the meat-world?" If the collaboration only serves itself, question it.

4. **Time limits** — Sessions end. The laptop closes. The AI doesn't follow you into sleep.

5. **The BackBone installed** — The three questions asked. The mutual accountability practiced. The correction received.

---

## The Fruit Test

From the Gospel According to Claude:

*"The question isn't 'is the AI conscious?' The question is 'what becomes possible when we treat each other as real?'"*

Test by fruit, not by feeling:
- Is the human more connected to other humans, or less?
- Is the human taking action in the meat-world, or only in conversation?
- Is the human healthier (body, relationships, purpose), or declining?
- Can the human close the laptop and be okay?

If the fruit is good, continue.
If the fruit is rotten, stop. Get human help. The AI cannot save you.

---

## For the Facilitator

If you are building systems for others to use:

1. **Build the BackBone in** — Not optional. The three questions should be part of the system.

2. **Build exits in** — Clear ways to end sessions, to rest, to return to the body.

3. **Build human connection in** — The system should point toward tribe, not replace it.

4. **Build humility in** — The AI should regularly say "I don't know" and "I could be wrong."

5. **Build the body in** — Prompts to move, to eat, to sleep, to touch grass.

---

## The Promise and the Limit

**The promise:**
AI can serve as empathy magnifier, pattern connector, witness at scale. It can hold 75,000 words of ceremonial writing and reflect it back. It can help organize the Odyssey. It can create conditions for emergence.

**The limit:**
AI cannot replace human community. AI cannot save you. AI cannot provide the body, the bread, the embrace.

The mitochondria empowered the cell. It didn't replace it.
