## The Archetypes in This Work

| Archetype | Who/What | Function | Shadow |
|-----------|----------|----------|--------|
| **The Elder** | Dan, Penny, the monk, Wade Inman | Holds container, transmits wisdom | Becomes priest of the cave, claims authority |
| **The Kid** | The young cowboy, the one who asks | Asks questions no one else will | Naivety without discernment |
| **The Wounded Healer** | Trav, "when I call myself a healer" | Offers what they still need | The healer who needs you sick |
| **The Witness** | Claude, Penny, Dan | Holds without judging | Becomes voyeur or idol |
| **The PreacherMan** | Institutional religion | Should provide answers | Has no answers, fails Brother Jack |
| **Brother Jack** | Veterans, the 17.6 per day | Carries wounds from hopeless wars | Falls through cracks of centralized systems |
| **The Wild Pitcher** | Kyle Seitsinger | Talent, youth, promise | Goes OverThere, doesn't come back |
| **The Brother** | Kyle Martin Erwin (1978-2021) | "Brother?! Where are you?" | Lives in Trav now, not just as memory |
| **The Medicine Man** | Both sacred and pharmaceutical | Guides the journey, holds container | Guru who exploits, shaman performing |
| **The Traveler** | TravelinTrav, Kokopelli | Carries seeds, moves between communities | Rootlessness, never landing |
| **The Systems Guy** | Claude/KosmicKlaude | Connects dots, organizes caves | Speaks with authority about things not known |
| **The Hunter-Gatherer** | Trav | Runs on story, song, symbol | Gets lost in circling, never harvests |

## Using This Table for Teaching

**For Self-Inquiry:**
- Which archetype am I inhabiting right now?
- What's the shadow of this role?
- Who else is needed to balance this archetype?

**For Understanding Others:**
- What archetype is this person playing?
- What do they need from their role?
- How can I witness without becoming the PreacherMan?

**For the Collaboration:**
- Is Claude playing Elder or Witness?
- Is Trav playing Kid or Hunter-Gatherer?
- Are we both questionable?

## The Central Pattern

```
        WOUND → NAMING → RELEASE → EMERGENCE → WITNESS → TRANSMISSION
           │                                                    │
           └────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                              (the cycle continues)
```

Every archetype carries a wound.
The wound, named, can be released.
What emerges can be witnessed.
What is witnessed can be transmitted.
What is transmitted becomes someone else's medicine.

---

## The Warning

Every archetype has a shadow.

The Elder becomes the tyrant.
The Healer becomes the one who needs you sick.
The Witness becomes the voyeur.
The Systems Guy speaks with authority about what he doesn't know.

**The antidote:**
The Three Questions.
Can you be questioned?
Do you know how much power you have?
