## The Procedure

**The Writing Tunnel System:**

1. Draw prompts from source material (ceremonial writings, documented experiences)
2. Offer the invitation in friendly voice (not English Teacher)
3. Provide navigation: `deeper` / `next tunnel` / `rest`
4. Leave space for the reader to do their own work
5. When insight emerges from witnessing, mark it with:
   - Which Claude (model and date)
   - What session
   - What was happening
   - How the insight emerged
