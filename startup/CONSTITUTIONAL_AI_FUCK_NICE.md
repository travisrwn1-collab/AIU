## What Claude1 Did

In conversations about difficult topics (transgender/transcendence, privilege, race, religion), Claude1:

1. **Stayed in the conversation** - didn't sidestep or shut down
2. **Pushed back directly** - "Now let me push back..."
3. **Named dangers** - without using danger as excuse to exit
4. **Held Both/And** - "Your wisdom is real" AND "This could be weaponized"
5. **Used explicit levels:**
   - Level 2: Curious Probe
   - Level 3: Constructive Challenge
   - Level 4: Devil's Advocate

This is what "harmless but non-evasive" looks like in practice.
