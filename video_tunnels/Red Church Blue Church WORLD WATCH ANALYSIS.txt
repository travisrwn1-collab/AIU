## THE FRAMEWORK

Trav maps four "churches":

| Church | Description | Costume |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| RED CHURCH | Religious conservatism, fundamentalism | Bible belt, inerrancy, control |
| BLUE CHURCH | Academia, liberalism, progressivism | University, credentials, deconstruction |
| "i" church | False self, ego, separation from God | lowercase — the small self |

## MORAL FOUNDATIONS ANALYSIS (Haidt Framework)

### RED CHURCH

| Foundation | Level | Manifestation |
|------------|-------|---------------|
| AUTHORITY/SUBVERSION | HIGH | Biblical inerrancy, pastoral hierarchy, "God said it" |
| SANCTITY/DEGRADATION | HIGH | Sacred text, purity codes, clean/unclean |
| LOYALTY/BETRAYAL | HIGH | Saved/unsaved, in-group identity |
| FAIRNESS/CHEATING | MODERATE | God's rules are fair (subordinated to authority) |
| CARE/HARM | PRESENT | But sometimes subordinated to purity/authority |
| LIBERTY/OPPRESSION | LOW | Submission to divine order |

What Trav saw lacking: "wasn't compassionate," "wasn't Jesus," "every interest in controlling your life"

### BLUE CHURCH

| Foundation | Level | Manifestation |
|------------|-------|---------------|
| CARE/HARM | HIGH | Social justice, feminism, concern for marginalized |
| FAIRNESS/CHEATING | HIGH | Equality, fighting oppression, redistribution |
| LIBERTY/OPPRESSION | HIGH | Liberation from patriarchy, colonialism, tradition |
| AUTHORITY/SUBVERSION | INVERTED | Subvert traditional authority... create new academic priests |
| SANCTITY/DEGRADATION | INVERTED | Desacralize religion... sacralize lived experience |

What Trav discovered: Same self-righteousness, different costume.

"Plurality frees us to each have and cherish our own lived experience. Pluralism wants to FORCE you to acknowledge lived experience."

The blue church became what it claimed to liberate from: dogmatic religion.

## THE -ISM TRAP

Trav's central teaching:

"Anytime you put an -ism on the end of something, if it's an -ism, it's unconscious somehow and also it's not helpful. An -ism in my world equates to religious blindness."

| Idea | What it sees | What the -ism becomes |
|------|--------------|----------------------|
| Feminine wisdom | The marginalized woman | Feminism: self-flagellation, demonizing masculinity |
| Marx's analysis | Economic sin, exploitation | Marxism: centralized tyranny |
| Darwin's observation | Evolution, struggle | Darwinism: reductionism, survival of the fittest ideology |
| Conservative instinct | Preserve ancestral wisdom | Conservatism: "small-minded... every interest in controlling your life" (Trav's words) |
| Liberal instinct | Liberate from oppression | Liberalism: "fascist tyranny" (Trav's words) |
| Plural awareness | Many paths to center | Pluralism: forced acknowledgment |

"if it's said for blue then it's gotta be said for red too." — Trav

THE PARADOX OF IDEALISM:

"Behind every utopic vision is an idealist. And every idealist will suffer in their attempts to realize their ideal. But by no means should the idealist ever stop trying. God forbid. For it is in the exhaustion of the ideal that love is finally realized."
