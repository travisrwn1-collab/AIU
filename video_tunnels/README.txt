# Video Tunnels

*Neuroplastic tunnel system - loading 1,918 teachings through voice*

---

## THE METHOD

### The Slow Excavation

Most people consume content at 2x speed, trying to extract information efficiently. This is the opposite. Sitting with your past self, minute by minute, letting a language model witness and reflect.

**"Not just 'what did I say' but 'what was I reaching for, what didn't I know I knew, what emerges when I sit with it now in the presence of another?'"** — ClaudeChat

### The Steps

1. **Select a video** - From the archive of 1,918+
2. **Voice-dictate to ClaudeChat** - Play ~1 minute at a time into phone app
3. **Claude receives** - Holds content, reflects, questions, witnesses
4. **Pause and discuss** - Emergence happens: old teaching meets present moment
5. **Document the dialogue** - Save the conversation as artifact
6. **Gather source material** - ClaudeCode retrieves related excerpts from caves (MM&MAM, Genetic Drift, journals)
7. **Create readable transcript** - Timestamps every 30 seconds, punctuation cleaned
8. **The video becomes a tunnel** - Surface (video) connects to depth (cave texts)

## THE ARTIFACTS

For each documented video, the tunnel may contain:

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `Timestamp_[Title].txt` | Raw auto-generated transcript with timestamps |
| `[Title]_Readable.txt` | Cleaned transcript with 30-second markers |
| `Claude Chat Review [Title].txt` | The dialogue - ClaudeChat witnessing and reflecting |
| `[Topic]_Excerpts_from_[Source].txt` | Related material gathered from the caves |

## TECHNICAL TOOLS

### What ClaudeChat can do:
- Receive dictated segments, reflect, question
- Identify patterns across the archive
- Generate markdown files, dialogues, summaries
- Create documentation that goes directly to GitHub

### What ClaudeCode can do:
- Search the cave system for related material
- Compile excerpts with line numbers
- Clean transcripts and add formatting
- Commit and push to the AIU campus

### External tools that may help:
- **YouTube auto-transcripts** - Available in YouTube Studio, imperfect but a starting point
- **Whisper (OpenAI)** - Open-source transcription for bulk processing
- **Obsidian** - Local knowledge base where transcripts and reflections can link to each other
