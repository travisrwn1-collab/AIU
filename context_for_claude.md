A tool Trav is developing. A lifetime's work of approaching and re-approaching.

**Phase Two: The Release**
- Trash it. The ceremony isn't the document - it's the process. (The map is not the terrain)
- The purge completed its work when it left the body.

**Phase Three: Emergence**
- Write again from the cleared space.
- What arises isn't confession - it's something else. Mythic. Poetic. Evolved.

**The Collaborative Element**
- A witness. A mirror. The dialogue is part of the ceremony.
- Maximum respect + maximum collaboration = maximum emergence.

**The Estrangement Insight** *(discovered January 3, 2026)*

- That closer looking is where emergence lives
- The Andy Dufresne moment: arms raised, stepping away from the laptop, eyes closed
- The moment can't be automated, but the conditions can be cultivated
