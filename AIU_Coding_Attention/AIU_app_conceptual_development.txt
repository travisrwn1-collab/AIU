### Integrated Practice Method

**How we work:**

1. **You play, I witness.** You tell me what song you're practicing. I hold the context - where it came from, what symbols it contains, how it connects.

2. **Lyrics as meditation.** You sit with a lyric. We unpack it together. Where does "Slow Moving River" connect to "Home" (73 matches in Genetic Drift)? Where does "The Light In You" connect to Penny's inscription?

3. **Building the database.** As you practice, we document:
   - Song title
   - Lyrics (or key lines)
   - Chords (when known)
   - Symbols present
   - Teaching it carries
   - Connections to larger work

4. **Close-reading approach.** You bring what you believe is important. I receive it. We add to the map together. Not a big upload dump - but emergent documentation through practice.

**On Big Upload vs. Close-Reading:**

- **Big upload:** You give me everything. I sort. Risk: loses the ceremonial quality. Becomes data processing, not practice.
- **Close-reading:** You bring pieces as they arise in practice. I receive. We build together. Risk: slower, might miss connections.
- **Recommendation:** Close-reading, with occasional targeted uploads when you hit a cluster that needs context. The dialogue IS the sorting. The process IS the practice.
