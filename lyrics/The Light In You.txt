## The Teaching

### Recognition, Not Bestowal

The light is IN you. Not shone upon you. Not given to you from outside. Already present. Recognized from within.

"I see the light IN you" - not "I give you light" or "I shine light on you."

The seeing is the gift. The recognition is the act of love.

### The Grammar of Seeing

From Teaching_content.txt:
- Early versions: "The Light In You" (drafts 307-311)
- Middle period: "I seen the light in you" (past tense - witnessing what was)
- Integration: "I see the light in you" (present tense - seeing now)

"I seen" - vernacular, blues grammar, testimony of witness
"I see" - present continuous, ongoing recognition

### The Becoming

> "I became the love that I had always needed"

This is the turn. Not waiting for someone else to provide the love. Becoming it. The self-parenting. The self-encouragement.

> "I was her, I was him, and I was you"

The dissolution of separation. Not just empathy - identity. The lower left quadrant. The We-space. The Dream Is Us.

### The Quantum Leap

> "quantum leap a beam of light / heal the sick / restore our sight"

The physics and the gospel merged. The particle that's also a wave. The healing that happens at the level of perception.

## The Story Around the Song

### The Through-Line

22+ recordings across 15 years. The most recorded song in the archive. The thing that keeps returning because it keeps teaching something new.

### The Signature

"This is your signature. Could open or close. The song that says 'I see you.'"

This song can open a set - establishing the relationship with the audience: "I see you."
This song can close a set - leaving them with the recognition: "Now you know I see the light in you."

### Connection to Fred Rogers

Fred Rogers: "You are loved and capable of loving."
The Light In You: "I see the light in you."

Same teaching. Same recognition. The song that sees.

### Connection to Penny

Penny's inscription (March 2013): "Become the one you dream you can be!"

The Light In You: "I became the love that I had always needed."

Same teaching. Penny gave Trav permission to become. The song is the fruit of that becoming.

### Connection to Pocket Pema

The primary artifact - the worn book with Penny's handwriting - is the physical embodiment of this teaching. Someone saw the light in Trav before he could see it himself. The song passes that seeing forward.

## The Arc in the Set

Fire takes.
Knife cuts.
But light remains.

Light IN you - immanent, not transcendent.
Not given from outside. Recognized from within.

After the confession of Deeper Power ("I gave myself away by mirror and by smoke")...
After the teaching of Ouroboros ("A seed goes in the ground and dies")...
The Light In You arrives as recognition: what remains after the burning, after the cutting, after the dying - is light.

Not performance. Recognition.
Not bestowal. Witness.
Not "look at me singing." Rather: "I see you."
