## Teaching Connection (January 3, 2026)

**The Nadu Teaching:**
Nadu - second wife, self-proclaimed Bruja - gave Trav the view of his victimhood he needed to get over himself. She helped him see how he gives himself away.

The song holds both edges:
- **The wound:** "I am humbled. I am broke. I gave myself away by mirrors and by smoke. Gospel man with feet of clay."
- **The medicine:** "Rise up from within (my Deeper Power). I become this loving-kindness, once again."

Giving yourself away as loss becomes giving yourself away as surrender.

"I was exactly as honest with them as I was capable of being honest with myself."

Connected song: "Ain't nobody ever gonna love me the way you did"
