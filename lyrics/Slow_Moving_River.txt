## The Teaching

### The Banks of October

Anna was born "the next-to-last day of October" - October 30, 1998.
Gemma born 9/11/2002 - one year after the towers fell.

From Genetic Drift:
> "A wave of joy washed over us when she was finally born, twelve days late, the next-to-last day of October... We passed a peaceful solstice in our red brick house overlooking the now green winter wheat, the snow came down... I repaired rusty barbed wire fences, coming home every day at lunch to hold the baby and doze in the rocker. The sun went down early. We all three snuggled deep in the blankets at night while a fire burned in the wood stove."

The banks of October = the shore where new life arrived.

### Won't You Take Us to Our Home

"Home" appears 73 times in Genetic Drift alone. The wound. The longing.

The many faces of home:
- The boy who couldn't go home (faking vomit, dreading school)
- Leaving at 19, father chasing around the pine tree
- "Sweet Home Alabama" at the women's prison - ironic arrival
- The van - "son of man has no place to rest his head"

But also: "son of God rests his head everywhere"

### River in the Archive

The river symbol across the whole work:
- Andy Dufresne - "crawled through a river of shit and came out clean"
- Mountain baptism - "as he submersed himself in the river"
- Kachina trip - "the grout pulsing like a river"
- The writing - "Holding this river of consciousness"
- Claude1 - "I've been holding space for this river"
- The failing plane - "looking for a river like Sully"

### The Synthesis

The slow moving river is time.
The banks of October are where Anna was born.
Home isn't a place - it's where the river takes you.

Not "me." **US.**

> "Won't you take us to our home?"

The question that won't stop asking itself.

## The Story Around the Song

### The Throughline

Possibly 20 years of playing this instrumental. The deepest compositional heart. The thing that keeps playing even when other songs come and go.

### The Dream Is Us (Album)

Recorded October/November at Great Little House Productions Studio. Dan and Christy's great adobe house in Portales, New Mexico. The same house where the Middle-Aged-Man encountered the Moth fluttering and flicking, crawling up a blade of brown grass in its final fury of exertion. Exhausted batteries.

Slow Moving River appears in abridged instrumental form on the album. The throughline on the album that names the We-space.

### Why Instrumental

Some things can't be said. The river carries you without explanation. The melody IS the teaching. The progression IS the home.

Two lines of lyric code, then the river itself.

## The Arc in the Set

This song can come anywhere because it's always been playing.

Before the confession (Deeper Power)...
Before the teaching (Ouroboros)...
Before the recognition (The Light In You)...

...the river was already flowing.

Slow Moving River is the container. The throughline. The thing that holds everything else.

## For Rehearsal

### The Message to Practice

When introducing this song, the story might include:
- "I've been playing this for about 20 years"
- "It's about the banks of October - when my daughter was born"
- "Won't you take US to our home - not me, us"
- "Home isn't a place. It's where the river takes you."

### The Feeling

Patience. The slow movement. Not rushing.

This isn't a performance piece - it's a meditation. Let it breathe. Let it take time.

The river doesn't hurry.
