# We Will Pray

We will pray for everybody
We will pray for everyone
We will pray for everybody
In the name of the Son

We will pray
We will pray

We will reach out to each other
We will touch that sacred hand
We will dance a holy rhythm
when we join that Big-Bond-Band

We will pray
We will pray

We sing out hallelujah
We praise a holy name
We smile at one another
in the fire each aflame

We will pray
We will pray

We will pray for those who hurt us
We will love our enemies
We will pray when we are walking
When we fall down on our knees

We praise him when we rise up
We praise him when we fall
We work out our salvation
God is with us through it all

We will pray
We will pray
We will pray
