## The Teaching

### The Non-Space (Little Big Man)

Like Little Big Man - living in both worlds and neither world. The non-space.

Living with the skin color of the oppressor. The middle-aged white man - the most shamed face in all the world. Not that we're the worst. Just that now we're recording it all and watching it happen over and over.

Like musical chairs - caught in the violence when the film started rolling.

### Born Into Sin

> "Bible Belt, born again / freed from my sin / 'cept that I was born into"

The genetic drift. The inherited karma. You can be born again, freed from personal sin - but you're still born INTO the sin of the people.

Prodigal sons born to prodigal peoples.

### The Trail

> "I come from the people / who broke the native tongue / trail of tears / Oklahoma landrun"

The confession. Not "those people did it" - "I come from the people." First person. Ownership.

The United States didn't invent slavery - we perfected it. The lie that it ended with the Civil War. The land run that took what was promised.

### The Covert Elite

> "The covert elite cast their shadows on the wall / Red Church, Blue Church / we took a great fall"

Plato's cave. The shadows on the wall. The puppeteers hidden. Red Church, Blue Church - both caught in the same fall.

Charlie bled. Kash fell in line. The names of the moment, the headlines metabolized into lyric.

### The Paradox

> "Damn the water / dam the flood / bless the mother's virgin blood"

Damn (curse) the water AND dam (contain) the flood. Same sound, different meanings. The paradox of being born into sin and still blessing the mother's blood.

### I Gave'er All I Could Give'er

The refrain. Not "I gave her everything" - "I gave her all I COULD give'er."

The limitation is named. The capacity was finite. It was everything available, but everything available wasn't everything needed.

This is confession without self-destruction. Honest accounting. "I gave what I had. It wasn't enough. And it was everything."

## The Story Around the Song

### The Rio Grande

The dammed river. The border. The line that divides. Harvestin' sandhill on the Rio Grand - the hunt continues, crossing water, crossing land.

### The Wichita Mountains

> "them Wichita mountains are but hills / but hills!!!"

Oklahoma mountains. Not grand by Colorado standards - but hills that matter. The hardhat boys sinking drills, paying bills. The oil that was boiling in the red man's land.

### The Folk Tradition

Oh Susanna, Shady Grove, Mr. Dooley - the folk songs woven in. The American songbook that carries both beauty and blood.

"We hung our head" - the shame carried in the tradition itself.

---

## The Arc in the Set

This song is the confession of inheritance. Different from Deeper Power (personal confession) - this is COLLECTIVE confession.

I come from the people. We took a great fall. This land is your land, this land is mine.

The privileged prayer said rightly. Not "I'm one of the good ones." Just: I come from this. I gave what I could. It wasn't enough.

## For Rehearsal

### The Message to Practice

When introducing this song, the story might include:
- "This is about being born into something"
- "I come from the people who broke the native tongue"
- "I live in both worlds and neither world - like Little Big Man"
- "I gave her all I could give'er - which was everything I had, and not enough"

### The Feeling

Not guilt performance. Honest reckoning.

The shame of the middle-aged white man isn't because we're the worst. It's because now we're recording it. Musical chairs - caught when the music stopped.

The response isn't self-flagellation. It's: "I gave what I could. I'm still here. The sun is rising."
