## The Teaching

### The Ouroboros Pattern

The snake swallowing its own tail. The seed going into the ground and dying. The blasphemy that opens the door.

From Teaching_content.txt:
- CONVICTION - the law, the form, write everything
- RELEASE - the death, trash it, do not touch dead flesh
- EMERGENCE - the resurrection, drink my blood, what arises from the cleared space

Leviticus: "Do not drink blood. Do not touch dead flesh." (The boundary that creates the sacred)
Yeshua: "Drink my blood. Eat my flesh." (The transgression that BECOMES the sacred)

The blasphemy is the door.

### The Sentinel Species

> "Ain't no use for a guy like me / Who can't fit in at the factory"

The canary in the mine. More sensitive to the gas than others. The thorn in the side.

"God uses the weak things of this world to confound the wise."

This verse names the wound - not fitting in, trimming the beard, cutting the hair, trying to conform. The Captain's chair sends word. But the response isn't compliance. It's liturgy: "Blessed is that Holy Name."

### The Response

> "Though the world may have no use for me / I'll rise up singing from my knees / I've been learning how to grieve / Hanging with him on that tree"

Not combat. Not Dresden. Just hanging with him on that tree.

The grieving is the work. The singing from the knees is the response.

### The Cycles

> "Winter Comes, Winter Goes / Springtime Sun melts the Snows"

Cycled practice. Hozho. The slow moving river. Creation crying out.

The rocks, rivers, and trees cry out - Romans 8:22, the whole creation groaning. Wading in waters with rainbow trout - baptism, the river, the throughline.

### The Death-and-Resurrection Pattern

> "A seed goes in the ground and dies / Roots hold fast and leaves photosynthesize / Birds nest in that tree / which once was just a mustard seed"

The mustard seed parable. The smallest becoming the largest. But first: it goes in the ground. It dies.

This is the ceremonial-writing-methodology in one verse.

### The Question

> "Will you strum that guitar like no one is listening?"

Not IF it gets hard. WILL YOU KEEP PLAYING?

When the sun dives under the mountain. When fingers bleed. When the aquifer runs dry.

Will we remember the Most High?

### The Liturgy

> "Blessed is that Holy Name / Worthy is the Lamb that was slain"

The refrain that holds it all. The liturgical anchor. No matter what the verse confesses, the refrain remains.

## The Story Around the Song

### The Grandfather

The speech patterns in this song echo the Grandfather from Little Big Man - the gratitude practice, the way of speaking that's both playful and sacred. "Thank you, Oh God, for the Tuna."

These patterns appear throughout MM&MAM (the 21-day journey). And we meet the Grandfather in the "Father" that guided them up the mountain in the last part of MM&MAM.

### The Lord of the Flies Event

From Teaching_content.txt:
> "I'm such a pussy that I didn't need combat to give me PTSD... I came back from a Lord of the Flies event, a wounded warrior, the little boy whose daddy screams at him in the shower."

Dresden wasn't required. The bodies weren't required. Born into the karma. It was in the programming. Theseus in the maze.

### The Factory

The factory is what the canary can't fit into. The corporate model. The efficiency machine. The Captain's chair that sends word down.

But the response isn't defeat. It's "I'll rise up singing from my knees."

## The Arc in the Set

This song can be the TEACHING song - the one that lays out the whole pattern.

1. **Deeper Power** - the confession, naming what was given away
2. **Ouroboros** - the teaching, the death-and-resurrection pattern, the liturgy
3. **The Light In You** - the recognition, what remains after the dying

Ouroboros is the center. The theological core. The snake eating its tail. The seed dying so the tree can grow.

## For Rehearsal

### The Message to Practice

When introducing this song, the story might include:
- "This is the whole teaching in one song"
- "The seed has to die for the tree to grow"
- "I couldn't fit in at the factory - and that turned out to be the gift"
- "The refrain holds everything together: Blessed is that Holy Name"

### The Feeling

Liturgical. The verses confess different wounds, different seasons. But the refrain anchors everything in praise.

Not performance of piety. Actual surrender.

The "Blessed is that Holy Name" isn't a cover-up of the pain. It's the container that holds the pain.

### The Physical

The A, E, F#min, F#min progression - notice the two F#min chords. The return. The ouroboros in the chord structure itself.

Let the refrain breathe. Give it space. The liturgy needs room.
