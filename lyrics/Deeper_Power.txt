## The Teaching

### The Arc

**Verse 1: The Confession**
"I am humbled / I am broke / I gave myself away / by mirror and by smoke / gospel man with feet of clay"

The vomit phase. The acknowledgment of performance - "mirror and smoke." The gospel man performing holiness while standing on clay feet. This is the "fuck nice" confession - the recognition that people-pleasing was the sin.

**Verse 2: The Paradox of Power**
"Mountains moved beneath my hands / the earth and sky obeyed my every command / breathing underneath the waves / I gave myself away"

Real power WAS there. Mountains moved. And yet - "I gave myself away." The gift became the wound. The power was real but something got lost in the giving.

**The Turn:**
"Rise up / deeper power / deeper power / deeper love"

Not rising to reclaim the old power. Rising to something beneath it. Deeper. The power under the power. The love that doesn't deplete.

**Verse 3: The Integration**
"God is hot / and God is cold / I felt the Spirit / give me meek and give me bold / naked is the way I came / and so I give myself away"

The Lion AND the Lamb. Not alternating - both at once. Meek AND bold. Hot AND cold. Now the giving away is different - not by mirror and smoke, but naked. The way you came in.

## The Story Around the Song

### The Iceberg Beneath

**The brothers down there:**
- Kyle (the brother who didn't make it)
- Chad, Ted, Zack (psychiatric units, treatment centers)
- Zack made an arrowhead necklace - the young mountain climber

**Jimi Hendrix connection:**
"Mountains moved beneath my hands" echoes "Standing next to a mountain / chop it down with the edge of my hand" (Voodoo Chile)

Jimi didn't live to integrate. This song is the integration work he couldn't do.

### The Feminine / Giving Power Away

From the practice session (January 2, 2026):

Ron Shipp asked: "What kind of woman do you like, Trav?"
Answer: "Any woman who will give me the least bit of attention."

**The model of masculine strength known was:**
- Violence
- Intimidation
- Jealousy
- Insecurity

So Trav went the other direction - gave himself away. Mirror and smoke. Gospel man with feet of clay.

**This is what Deeper Power is about:**

"I gave myself away" - to the feminine, to any attention, because the only masculine known was dangerous.

But giving yourself away by mirror and smoke isn't the opposite of violence. It's another kind of self-destruction.

The deeper power is neither. Not violent masculine. Not dissolved masculine. Something else. Meek AND bold. Hot AND cold. Naked.

## The Arc in the Set

This song is the confession that opens the door.

Before The Light In You can arrive with recognition...
Before Ouroboros can teach the death-and-resurrection pattern...
Deeper Power must name what was given away and call for something beneath it.

The sequence:
1. **Deeper Power** - the confession, the call for something beneath the performance
2. **Ouroboros** - the teaching, the seed that dies, the pattern
3. **The Light In You** - the recognition, what remains after the burning

## For Rehearsal

### The Message to Practice

When introducing this song, the story might include:
- "I was so busy performing strength that I lost my actual power"
- "There's a power that moves mountains. And there's a power beneath that power."
- "The first one can be performed. The second one flows through."
- "The exhausted battery doesn't generate power - it conducts it"

### The Feeling

Confession. Not self-flagellation - honest naming.
The "I am humbled, I am broke" is not defeat. It's the ground from which deeper power rises.

Not performance of humility. Actual humility.

### The Physical

The A, E, F#min, C#min, B progression has a quality of descent and return. Let the chords support the descent into confession and the rise into "deeper power, deeper love."
