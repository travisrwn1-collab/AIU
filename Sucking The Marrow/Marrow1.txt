                    EXTRACTION 3: THE VULNERABILITY FILE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Source: ceremonial_writings/January_6_2026_Initiation_Vulnerability_Backbone.txt
Lines: 79-89

I believe that vulnerability is an essential element of love. I believe that
true love, and true vulnerability must be a conscious decision. We make
ourselves vulnerable by choice. If we make ourselves vulnerable without
conscious choice, it is just naive. Naive is okay; we learn from it.

I write about myself in this public venue because vulnerable sharing is
natural for me. I am an autobiographical content creator. I have worked for
many years, making many mistakes along the way, to be able to do what I do.
I have over-shared. I have taken my audience's response (and lack of
response) personally. I have been naive.

I make myself vulnerable to my reader because of love, not because I am
naive about what you might do with my decision to make myself vulnerable. I
write about self-experience because I believe that in order to know anything
outside ourselves, we must first come to know the learner. Because all
knowledge is filtered through the self-experience, self-knowledge is
essential. My work as an autobiographer is only valuable insofar as it helps
you to see yourself.

I once sent a manuscript of mine to a writer who had inspired me. He gave me
some of the most useful feedback I have ever received. He told me that when
he reads, he is reading for his own healing, and that my work was not helping
him to heal. He told me that a lot of the details that I considered important
were not serving the reader. In other words, I was over-sharing. He suggested
that I scrap my writing project and start again, and that is exactly what I
eventually did. I rewrote the book with a completely different approach.

It's good to know, for myself, that I am open to criticism. I am teachable.
The advice that I got from that writer was hard to swallow. Putting your art
out there, putting your heart out there, is a very vulnerable process.
Sometimes it seems like everyone knows exactly what I need. And sometimes it
just seems like no one cares at all about my work. I trust that when my music
and my writing is/becomes/can-be-experienced-as healing and helpful that it
will be successful, and I have prayed that it will not be successful before
then. Until then, I will just keep giving my best love to my audience by
presenting to you what I have to offer.

CONNECTION TO CURRENT ARC: Conscious vulnerability vs naive vulnerability.
The difference between Del (naive) and Trav (conscious). BaCkBoNe.
