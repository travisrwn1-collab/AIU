STRUCTURE

Day 1 opens as a distress call - an email to Eileen and Dave at Muse Harbor Publishing. The middle-aged man has eaten mushrooms. Instead of sinking into the soil, he writes. "Please pardon the draftiness, dear reader, after all, this is a distress call."

The document moves between:
- Third person narrative (the middle-aged man)
- First person intrusions (where the writer breaks frame)
- Dialogue with the Muse (a feminine presence who guides, questions, seduces, holds)
- Email exchanges (real texts with ex-wife, brother, father)
- Poetry interludes (Hafiz, original songs)

---

THE ARC

The Nigredo: Violence toward animals. Father's homophobia. Self-hate made physical. The marriage that became a prison. "One of the top five self-bludgeoners" Penny had ever met.

The Descent: The grass. The soil. The saliva pooling. Becoming the serpent. "He was one now with all he'd ever known. He was the Christ. He was the one who crucified the Christ."

The Vision: Emma Kate as pure healing. The Christ Daughter arising. Serpent children being born on beaches. The great kundalini unwinding.

The Baptism: Cold mountain stream, 40 degrees. Stripping to underwear. The immersion. Running from the approaching car. "Father, empower me to follow. Make me a disciple."

The Mountain: The dialogue with the Father restored. "Can I climb that mountain?" "Yes son. You can try. Just take it one step at a time. Let's do it together."

THE PROTO-PARENTHETICAL

The methodology is already here - raw, unstructured, but present. The narrator constantly interrupts himself:

  "(No. He didn't actually imagine himself as Han. that's the voice that I'm making peace with here. literary voice. fictional voice...)"

  "end draft? again? already? when the veil is torn between that which is written and that which can't be wrote?"

This IS the parenthetical practice before it had a name. The writer watching himself write. The consciousness commenting, looking back on itself.
