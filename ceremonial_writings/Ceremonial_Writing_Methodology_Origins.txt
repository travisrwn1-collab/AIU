# Ceremonial-Writing-Methodology: Origins

## How It Began

Thank you for snakes.

Google searches about snakes and symbolism led to Jung - the Red Book, the conversation with archetypes. Jung said: "It's okay to have this conversation."

But before Jung, there was the practice.

---

## The Shamanic Journey Practice

**The Book:** Paul Francis, "The Shamanic Journey: A Practical Guide to Therapeutic Shamanism"
*(See: AIU_Collage/Collage material/Shamanic_Journey_Paul_Francis.jpg)*

This book taught self-guided journeys through the imaginative realm. Meditative work done BEFORE the sacred medicine journeys.

**The Location:** Johnson Lake. The campsite where Trav went alone. The night he was scared and left the tent. The cougar skulking fear. The Gollum fear. The clicking in the woods.

**The Entrance:** The axis mundi hole by the campsite. The spiral down.

---

## The Practice: Hollowing Out

> "I do not have to make anything happen. I must trust snake."

The methodology before it had a name:
- Let go of agenda
- Stay hollowed out
- Let spirit follow snake
- Trust that healing is happening even below consciousness

> "Snake just needs me to stay hollowed out. It's okay if I don't see all of this like a movie. It is all happening even below my consciousness."

---

## Meeting Snake

**August 31, 2019** - First documented journey
*(See: Lyrics/Shamanic_Journey_Snake.txt)*

Snake on the large rock behind the campsite. Jump into snake. Flying toward the axis mundi hole. Spiraling downward.

> "Show me how to walk in this world snake. Show me how to heal and be healed."

Snake doesn't want worship. Snake needs you hollowed out.

**The Ceremony Place of Snake Clan:**
- Towering snake
- Black smoke tendrils
- Whiteness/glowing of ancestors in spirit form

---

## The Warning

The last time at the axis mundi, a cougar grabbed before entering, and snake was chopped in half entering the hole. So he stopped.

The practice has teeth. It can go wrong. Respect is required.

---

## Becoming One with Snake

**September 6, 2019** - Second documented journey
*(See: Lyrics/Shamanic_Journey_Snake.txt)*

> "Snake wraps himself around me. My head is sticking out of his coils. He gets tighter. This is how we become one tonight. I am embraced. I am safe."

Snake as embrace, not threat. The constriction as safety, not death.

**Meeting Dragon:**
In the axis mundi, Dragon waits. Can never see his head. Melting into Dragon. Flying above clouds through Dragon's eyes.

**Healing Journey for Stan:**
Flying in and around a friend's head, trusting that dragon/snake is doing healing work. "I do not know what it is. I just fly and let whatever it is that is being done be done."

## The Ouroboros Emerges

From this practice - the snake relationship, the spiraling, the death-and-rebirth pattern - the ouroboros symbol emerged.

The snake eating its tail. The seed going into the ground and dying. The ceremonial-writing-methodology in symbolic form.
