# Visiting Lucy

*Sept 8th 2019 shamanic journey*
*Extracted from Visiting Lucy.docx*
*January 6, 2026*

---

I enter with the intent of meeting Lucy and possibly meeting her tribe. I go to Axis mundi location. Snake is coiled around me this time. This seems to be the sign to wait, checking that I am ready to enter, to become one with snake, to enter axis mundi. We enter axis mundi and start our way back down. We are at snake clan ceremony area. Dragon is there. Snake rolls in a wheel and rolls around dragon. Snake shoots straight into the heart of dragon. We take flight. On our way to find lucy. Flying above the clouds. Now we land in a clearing surrounded by large trees. Green grass. This is where lucy is. I sit in meditation in the clearing, waiting for her people to approach. They approach cautiously 3 feet tall. I acknowledge them. They acknowledge me. There is one with a baby. I become the baby. It is how I am welcomed into their presence? I just want to sit with them. I want to know them. They all sit around me. Crouching. sitting on bottoms. what is it like to be with you? What is it like to be you? I need to be a part of a group. My back is hurting in the middle world. It is taking much concentration to maintain my posture. I am exhausted. I lose connection. I must trust. The drumming slows down. I begin thanking everyone. Dragon. Snake. Lucy. Please help me to be a part. Here. I need to be here. I need this place. I need to feel connection to lower world.
