# Haiku

*Extracted from Haiku.docx*
*January 6, 2026*

---

The dandelions
Gold and green in summertime
Soft umbrella saints

I catch quick glimpses
The women, I see their flesh
No need to deny

There's no need at all
Perfectly normal for us
Human animals

Pink inside our flesh
Yet trace immortality
Just enough for shame

line quite thin yet strong
If one chooses to divide
Divine brain creatures

How religion serves
Still some mystery to us
Creator of gods

Our God's jealousy
The rip of dogma?
Submit to authority

A deadly supine
Safety of retreat?
Institutional

Father's approval
Taken in order
Our God's jealousy

Learned by rote and rhyme
Fundamentalist
Label the infinite sky

Critical unthought
