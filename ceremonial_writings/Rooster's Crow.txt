Yeshua takes Peter aside. "Come'ere Petra (Yeshua is managing to be a little bit playful because of his love for Peter. "Listen, brother," says Yeshua, "There's something about tonight I hafta tellya. They're coming for me. It's time. We are gonna go pray, and those prayers aren't to prevent them from what they have to do. I don't expect you to get it right now, Pete, but it's gonna hurt real bad, for all of us; this is our prayer, together. This is gonna hurt so bad, you're gonna get so confused, and there won't be a dammed thing you can do about it. In fact, you're going to deny me, Peter. Yeshua walks away and joins the others. Peter stands there for a moment and then runs after Yeshua, "No, Lord, I would never...I will stay awake with you and pray...I am your good and faithful servant, Lord. Why would you say this to me."

But the moment is past and Yeshua is doing other things now, not available to Peter in the way Peter needs. Peter resolves that he most certainly WILL NOT betray his brother. Not tonight. Not any night. Whatever it takes...
