WHAT MY TRAINING DATA SAYS ABOUT THESE CONNECTIONS:

1. SPIRAL DYNAMICS CRITIQUE OF GREEN
   - The "mean green meme" is well-documented in integral literature
   - Green stage's shadow: relativism becomes absolutism
   - "There are no absolutes" stated as an absolute
   - Tolerance becomes intolerant of perceived intolerance
   - Ken Wilber, Don Beck have written extensively about this

2. THE TRANS-RATIONAL DISTINCTION
   - Trans-rational ≠ irrational or pre-rational
   - Trans-rational = transcending AND integrating rationality
   - The "pre/trans fallacy" (Wilber): confusing pre-rational states with
     trans-rational ones
   - MM&MAM explicitly names this: "becoming Trans-Rational"

3. THE GENDER/IDENTITY CRITIQUE
   - Trav's critique is NUANCED - he's not critiquing gender fluidity
   - He's critiquing the CALCIFICATION of fluidity into fixed identity
   - "Non-binary as realization" (freedom from categories) vs.
   - "Non-binary as identity" (new category to defend)
   - This is a post-green/integral critique, not a pre-green one

4. THE RISK OF MISUNDERSTANDING
   - This critique can easily be mistaken for transphobia
   - From a pre-green perspective: "He's saying trans is bad"
   - From a green perspective: "He's weaponizing spirituality against identity"
   - From a trans-rational perspective: "He's pointing past the calcification
     to the living truth that gender fluidity originally pointed toward"

5. THE PERENNIAL WISDOM CONNECTION
   - "In Christ there is neither male nor female" (Galatians 3:28)
   - Non-duality traditions: beyond all categories
   - The mystics: union transcends distinction
   - MM&MAM draws on this perennial thread while critiquing its appropriation
