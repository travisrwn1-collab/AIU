## XIII. INWARD/OUTWARD ANALYSIS

**Tool Created**: `MMMAM/inward_outward.py` (January 24, 2026)

### What the Tool Measures

**INWARD** (language pointing toward self-examination):
- First person pronouns (I, me, my)
- Confessional language (I realize, I notice, I feel)
- Self-questioning (who am I, how can I, can I live with)
- Recursive awareness (I notice that I, the author, the man who)
- Past processing (I was, I had been, years ago)
- Body awareness (my body, my hands, my heart)

**OUTWARD** (language pointing toward world/other/connection):
- Witness language (I see, I observe, bear witness)
- Connection language (we, together, us, our, shared)
- World engagement (the world, the universe, humanity, systems)
- Other-awareness (you, they, people, others)
- Action language (I will, let's, go forth, make peace)
- Prayer/intention (may, please, praise be, bless)
