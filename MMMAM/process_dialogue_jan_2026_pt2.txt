CLAUDE (Web Search Results, January 21, 2026):

1. ANTHROPIC'S CORPORATE STRUCTURE

Public Benefit Corporation with Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT)

Anthropic operates as a Public Benefit Corporation. The key structural element
is the Long-Term Benefit Trust, which holds a special class of shares (Class T)
with escalating board-election rights. Over time, the LTBT will elect a majority
of directors - giving an independent body of technical/safety experts oversight
of strategic decisions.

The LTBT's purpose is "the responsible development and maintenance of advanced
AI for the long-term benefit of humanity." As of October 2025, the Trust members
are: Neil Buddy Shah, Kanika Bahl, Zach Robinson, and Richard Fontaine.

Major Investors:
- Amazon: $8B total
- Google: $3B (14% stake, capped at 15%, no voting rights, no board seats)
- Microsoft: up to $5B
- Nvidia: up to $10B
- Plus: ICONIQ, Lightspeed, Fidelity, Salesforce Ventures, Menlo Ventures,
  Bessemer, Qatar Investment Authority

Valuation: $183B (September 2025 Series F), estimated $350B as of November 2025.
Potential IPO in 2026.

2. ANTHROPIC-PALANTIR TIES

November 2024: Anthropic partnered with Palantir and AWS to provide U.S.
intelligence and defense agencies access to Claude models. Claude became
available in Palantir's defense-accredited environment at Impact Level 6 (IL6) -
used for classified defense data.

April 2025: Partnership expanded through Palantir's FedStart program. Claude
became available to "millions" of federal workers. Now offered at FedRAMP High
and DoD Impact Level 5 security standards. Google Cloud hosts Claude in this
arrangement (multi-cloud strategy).

What they're doing: Claude 3 and 3.5 models are integrated into Palantir's AI
Platform (AIP) for intelligence analysis, automatic complex data processing,
and "operational efficiency" for defense/intelligence agencies.
