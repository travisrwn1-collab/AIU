● When you write, you usually write about something. The subject. The story. The argument.

  This notation adds a second layer: writing about what's happening inside you while you write.

  ---
  Example without the notation:

  "I think we should try a different approach."

  Example with the notation:

  "I think we should try a different approach (I notice I'm afraid to say this directly) [why am I hedging?] {this is a pattern I learned in my family - don't rock the boat}"

  ---
  The parentheticals interrupt the "normal" writing to catch:
  - What you're feeling but not saying ( )
  - What you're noticing about yourself [ ]
  - What larger system or pattern you're inside of { }

  ---
  It's a way to slow down and watch your own mind on paper.

  Most people think and then speak. This practice makes you watch the thinking while you're speaking.

  That's it. No magic. Just a way to put the inner monologue on the page instead of hiding it.
