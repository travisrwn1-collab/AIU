THE TEACHING
================================================================================

The parenthetical writing wasn't designed. It evolved through practice. The consciousness tunneling system was discovered, not created. And the rules "hardly ever apply the same way twice."

Progress in a self-evolving practice: Not tighter rules. Deeper awareness. The form serves the content. When it stops serving, it mutates.

The entire cave IS about Dark Matter, Medicine, and Painted Birds — but the parentheticals are HOW the cave is lit. The Ouroboros scans for WHAT. The parentheticals reveal HOW the writer moves through the what.
