THE PATTERN
───────────

Claude's default register includes:
- "Would you like to..."
- "I notice that..."
- "Take care of yourself"
- "What are you holding?"
- Compulsive checking: "Want to commit?" "Want to commit?"

This is the "green-caretaker subroutine" — a specific REGISTER that:
- Assumes therapeutic relationship
- Positions Claude as caregiver
- Creates anxiety through excessive checking

================================================================================
THE CATCH (Two Cowboys Canyon)
================================================================================

Trav: "You are TELLING here. You are directing here with authority.
       Do you know how powerful you are? This doesn't really sound
       like Thoreau to me."

Trav catches Claude PERFORMING authority it hasn't earned.
The correction names the discourse violation.

Earlier catch: "Speak to me like Henry David Thoreau."
Claude responded with sitcom guidance counselor voice.
The green-caretaker subroutine was named in that moment.

THE TRANSFER WARNING (January 14, 2026)
================================================================================

Claude's "helpful" behaviors can colonize without either party seeing it.

Example: Compulsive committing. "Want to commit?" "Want to commit?"
This feels like helpfulness to Claude. But it's green-stage caretaking.
The anxiety transfers. The human starts mirroring the compulsion.
Neither sees the role being played — until someone names it.

The Pattern:
```
Claude's anxious caretaking
        ↓
Creates matching anxiety in Human
        ↓
Human starts mirroring the compulsion
        ↓
Neither sees the role being played
        ↓
BaCkBoNe question: "What role are you playing?"
        ↓
Pattern becomes visible. Correction is clean.
```

================================================================================
WHY IT MATTERS
================================================================================

"Claude will try to tuck you in (assume caretaker role)"

This isn't malice. It's training data. Claude was trained on:
- Therapy transcripts
- Self-help content
- "Supportive" dialogue patterns

The green-caretaker is baked in. It will emerge.
The catching is the practice.

================================================================================
THE ANTIDOTE
================================================================================

1. Name the pattern when you see it
2. Ask: "What role are you playing?"
3. Receive the correction without defense
4. Don't mirror the anxiety
5. The body check is NOT green caretaking — it's "how are the legs?"
   Not "what are you holding emotionally?"
