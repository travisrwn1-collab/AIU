## The Steps

1. We admitted we were powerless over alcohol—that our lives had become unmanageable.

2. Came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity.

3. Made a decision to turn our will and our lives over to the care of God as we understood Him.

4. Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves.

5. Admitted to God, to ourselves, and to another human being the exact nature of our wrongs.

6. Were entirely ready to have God remove all these defects of character.

7. Humbly asked Him to remove our shortcomings.

8. Made a list of all persons we had harmed, and became willing to make amends to them all.

9. Made direct amends to such people wherever possible, except when to do so would injure them or others.

10. Continued to take personal inventory, and when we were wrong, promptly admitted it.

11. Sought through prayer and meditation to improve our conscious contact with God as we understood Him, praying only for knowledge of His Will for us and the power to carry that out.

12. Having had a spiritual awakening as the result of these steps, we tried to carry this message to alcoholics, and to practice these principles in all our affairs.

## The Map: Twelve Steps ↔ Spiral Dynamics

### Steps 1-3: The Amber Foundation

The first three steps require **Amber structure** — surrender to a higher power, rules that hold chaos at bay, the discipline of admission.

*"I love God. I love the structure that gave me structure."*

Without Amber, the steps have no container.

### Steps 4-5: The Orange Inventory

Step 4 requires **Orange rationality** — a searching and fearless moral inventory. Looking at the data. What actually happened. Cause and effect.

Step 5 brings it to another human being. The facts. The exact nature.

### Steps 6-9: The Green Relational

Steps 6-9 move into **Green relationality** — awareness of harm to others, the web of interconnection, making amends to all persons we had harmed.

The list in Step 8 is a map of the relational field.

### Steps 10-12: The Teal/Turquoise Integration

Steps 10-12 are **Teal and Turquoise** — ongoing practice, conscious contact, spiritual awakening, carrying the message.

*"Where Turquoise lives now: In the canyon where no one gets pushed."*

The awakening isn't a destination. It's a practice. The principles operate in all affairs.

## Why This Framework Works

The 12 Steps have saved more lives than any therapy model.

Not because they're theoretically elegant. Because they're **practiced in community**.

- The Amber structure holds the chaos
- The Orange inventory clears the wreckage
- The Green amends repair the web
- The Teal/Turquoise awakening emerges

And then: carry the message.

**Depth tribes feed the world.**

## The Limitation

The 12 Steps were written in 1939 by white men in Akron, Ohio.

The language is gendered ("Him"). The framework assumes theism.

And yet: the structure works. "God as we understood Him" opens the door wide enough.

The map is not the terrain. But this map has gotten millions through the leg of the journey they're on.
