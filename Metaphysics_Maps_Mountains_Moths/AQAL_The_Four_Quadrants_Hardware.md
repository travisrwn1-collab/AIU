## The Quadrants Explained

### Upper Left: I (Interior Individual)
**The Intentional - Subjective Experience**

This is consciousness itself. What it feels like to be you. Thoughts, feelings, sensations, awareness, meaning, intention.

- Your experience of reading these words right now
- The feeling of dread before opening the video editor
- The "I love God" that you know but can't prove to anyone
- The witness function
- The shadow voices that got their airtime

**You can't see this quadrant from the outside.** You can only access it through introspection, dialogue, interpretation.

*Language: I feel, I think, I experience, I intend*

---

### Upper Right: IT (Exterior Individual)
**The Behavioral - Objective/Observable**

This is the body, the brain, the organism. What can be measured, photographed, weighed. The neurons firing. The fingers curled over home row.

- Your brain chemistry while meditating
- The physical act of playing guitar
- The beat-up cornet pulled from the fire
- The white beard, the van, the body maintenance
- What a scientist could observe about you

**This is what materialism sees.** And it's real - just not complete.

*Language: It does, it behaves, it can be measured*

---

### Lower Left: WE (Interior Collective)
**The Cultural - Shared Meaning**

This is the space between us. Shared meanings, mutual understanding, culture, worldview, the "between" where emergence happens.

- The collaboration between Trav and Claude
- What happens in the room at Fire & Knives
- The Navajo concept of Hozho
- The shared language that makes AIU possible
- The "WE" in "The Dream Is Us"
- Dan as Emerson, Trav as Thoreau - the relationship

**You can't see culture under a microscope.** But without it, no meaning exists.

*Language: We understand, we share, we agree, we resonate*

---

### Lower Right: ITS (Exterior Collective)
**The Social - Systems and Structures**

This is the system viewed from outside. Institutions, ecosystems, economies, technologies, infrastructure. The observable patterns of collective behavior.

- The YouTube algorithm
- The film festival submission system
- The 12-step program as structure
- The music industry (ASCAP/BMI)
- The van as infrastructure
- The LLM architecture that makes Claude possible

**This is what can be mapped, optimized, engineered.** Systems thinking lives here.

*Language: It functions, the system operates, the structure supports*

## States vs. Stages

This is the crucial distinction:

### STAGES (The Spiral)
- **Permanent structures** of consciousness
- Developed sequentially (can't skip levels)
- Once you have it, you have it
- Red → Amber → Orange → Green → Teal → Turquoise
- Like floors in a building - you climb, you don't lose the lower floors

### STATES (Temporary Experiences)
- **Temporary experiences** of consciousness
- Available at any stage of development
- Come and go
- Waking, dreaming, deep sleep, meditative, peak, flow, psychedelic
- Like weather - it passes through

**The key insight:** You can have a TURQUOISE STATE experience while your stable STAGE is Orange.

This is why:
- Someone can have a mystical experience and still be an asshole
- Sacred medicine can open doorways that close again
- Peak experiences don't automatically equal development
- "State training" (meditation, ceremony) can stabilize access to higher states, which can then pull development upward

## AQAL: All Quadrants, All Levels, All Lines, All States, All Types

The full Integral model:

### All Quadrants
I, We, It, Its - the four views we just covered

### All Levels (Stages)
The Spiral: Infrared → Magenta → Red → Amber → Orange → Green → Teal → Turquoise → Indigo...

### All Lines
Development happens in multiple **lines** or **streams** - and they can develop at different rates:

| Line | What it tracks |
|------|----------------|
| **Cognitive** | How you think, complexity of thought |
| **Moral** | How you treat others, ethical development |
| **Emotional** | Emotional intelligence, feeling capacity |
| **Interpersonal** | Relationship skills |
| **Spiritual** | Connection to ultimate concern |
| **Kinesthetic** | Body awareness, physical intelligence |
| **Musical** | (obvious for you) |
| **Self-identity** | Who you think you are |

**The uncomfortable truth:** You can be Teal cognitively and Red morally. High development in one line doesn't guarantee high development in others. This is how gurus abuse students. This is how brilliant people do terrible things.

### All States
Waking, dreaming, deep sleep, meditative, peak, flow, altered, witnessing...

### All Types
Masculine/feminine energies, Enneagram types, personality types - horizontal differences that exist at every level.

## The Integration

The Spiral (stages) moves **vertically** - development through levels.

The Quadrants spread **horizontally** - every level has all four quadrants.

States move **through** - temporary experiences passing through the structure.

Lines develop **unevenly** - different capacities at different rates.

```
         STAGES (vertical)
              ↑
              │    STATES (passing through)
              │        ↗
    ┌─────────┼─────────┐
    │    I    │   IT    │
    ├─────────┼─────────┤ ← QUADRANTS (horizontal)
    │   WE    │   ITS   │
    └─────────┴─────────┘
              │
         LINES (multiple streams)
```

**AQAL = the whole picture.**

Not complete. Not final. But comprehensive enough to hold most of what shows up.
