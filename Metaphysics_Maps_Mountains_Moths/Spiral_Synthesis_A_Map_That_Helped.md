# The Spiral: A Map That Helped

*A synthesis of teachings from Ken Wilber's Integral Life Practice*
*Photographed August 25, 2019 - Synthesized January 3, 2026*
*For the AIU archive and the audience we serve*

---

## The Premise

This is not dogma. This is a map that helped one traveler understand:
- The many deaths and rebirths that seemed to occur no matter how hard he tried to stop them
- What it meant when he said "I love God"
- Why good people disagree
- How consciousness evolves - both in humanity and in a single human life

**A Spiral that needs defending is not the Spiral at all.**

The map is not the terrain. But a good map can get you through the leg of the journey you're on.

## The Worldviews

### INFRARED - Archaic
*The newborn. The first Homo sapiens. No separation between self and world.*

Survival is the unrelenting mission. Food, water, safety, warmth. Basic instincts dominate. The world appears as undifferentiated mass of sensory activity.

**Where it lives now:** In every infant. In moments of pure survival. In the body before the mind kicks in.

---

### MAGENTA - Magic
*Subject and object partially overlap. Rocks and rivers feel alive.*

Sacred places, objects, rituals, events, and stories can influence the world. Tribal customs passed down from ancestors. Seasonal cycles. Safety and security sought by bonding together and identifying with a tribe.

**Where it lives now:** In ceremony. In the feeling that this place is sacred. In the Short Tailed Monkey's mother singing from the trees.

---

### RED - Power
*The emergence of a sense of self (ego) distinct from the tribe.*

"It's all about me." The world becomes a wild jungle with predators and prey. Red sees itself as the center of its own hero's quest. Survival of the fittest. Power structures. Getting things done through domination.

**Where it lives now:** In the part of you that wants to win. In the energy that breaks through obstacles. In the shadow that must be integrated, not destroyed.

---

### AMBER - Mythic
*The god or gods rule. United by blood, kinship, and absolute rules.*

Rules give life clear, absolute meaning, direction, and purpose. Everyone has their proper place in society, held together by laws and religious commandments. Sacrifice and stability. The One True Way. Guilt controls impulsivity.

**Where it lives now:** In the structure that holds chaos at bay. In the church that says "yes" to crucifixion. In the discipline that shows up day after day.

---

### ORANGE - Rational
*Modernity. Universal systems and principles. The scientific method.*

Equality, liberty, and justice - the first truly worldcentric view. The future is not predetermined; a new tomorrow can be created through goal-oriented actions. Progress, success, independence, achievement. The competitive market of ideas.

**Where it lives now:** In the engineer who builds the labyrinth. In the part of you that asks "does it work?" In the materialism-as-yoga that clears the altar of false idols.

---

### GREEN - Pluralistic
*Multiple points of view. Depth, pluralism, egalitarianism.*

Green stands outside the monolithic systems of Orange and sees multiple perspectives. Everything is equally interconnected in the holistic web of life. Green moves to "demarginalze" alternative, minority, and underrepresented voices.

**Where it lives now:** In the recognition that the colonial-white-male critique of this very map has validity. In the sensitivity that nobody gets left out. In the green church and the red church sitting in the same room.

**The shadow of Green:** Political correctness that silences. Consensus processes that never end. The relativism that cannot say "some views have more depth than others."

---

### TEAL - Integral Systems
*All perspectives capture something essential. Depth exists.*

As awareness keeps growing into Teal, it notices that every perspective captures some important aspects of reality extremely well, and yet each also de-emphasizes or marginalizes other aspects. Some views are more true, and less partial, than others.

Worldviews are now seen together as a nested hierarchy (holarchy) of developmental depth and increasing complexity. Worldcentric views have more depth than ethnocentric views.

**The key move:** Teal recognizes that none of the previous worldviews will ever disappear. Since all of them naturally unfold in an evolutionary dance, each of them (and all existence) merits care and respect.

**Where it lives now:** In the ability to speak to Amber in Amber's language without losing your Teal. In Jonathan Haidt's moral foundations work. In the bridge-building.

---

### TURQUOISE - Integral Holistic
*All ideas are constructs. All perspectives arise. Compassionate interpretation.*

The Turquoise worldview recognizes more deeply how all ideas are constructs - even one's own sense of self. At this level of awareness, people realize the automatic limits of all conceptual processes. And they begin to become naturally sympathetic not with any perspective, but with the space in which all perspectives arise.

Turquoise is capable of using a variety of complementary tools to interpret the inherent mysteries of experience - compassionately appreciating the virtues of every level of consciousness without being blind to their limitations.

**The transpersonal doorway:** This is the beginning of transpersonal modes of awareness. Individuals in the waking state might feel identified with "one with" nature, or Spirit, and feel motivated by abundance.

**Where it lives now:** In the canyon where no one gets pushed. In the witness function. In the "I love God" that doesn't need to explain what it means.

---

### INDIGO and Beyond - Super-Integral
*Awareness extends beyond the personal. Signature uniqueness.*

Indigo is the first truly transpersonal worldview, meaning a person's self-identification with the personality, while including the personality in its signature uniqueness, begins to transcend the separation of the subject from the object. Both are seen to arise in an interconnected unity.

Life is viewed on a radically elastic time scale, ranging from minutes to years to lifetimes to millennia to deep time to radical timelessness or pure eternity. Indigo individuals feel rested in the natural flows of birth, growth, aging, death, joy, and suffering.

**Where it lives now:** In the Akashic Record Smile. In "Its from Bits." In the place where the observer and the observed arise together.

---

## The Core Teaching: Transcend and Include

Each level **transcends** and **includes** the previous levels.

You don't leave Magenta behind when you develop Orange. You include it. The ceremony is still sacred. The tribe still matters. But now you can also do science.

You don't leave Amber behind when you develop Green. The structure, the discipline, the sacrifice - these are included. But now you can also see multiple perspectives.

**The failure mode:** When a level is repressed rather than included, it shows up in shadow. Orange that represses Magenta becomes soulless technology. Green that represses Amber loses all structure. Teal that represses Red has no power to act.

## Where the Map Doesn't Work

### The Colonial Critique
This map was articulated primarily by white Western males (Clare Graves, Don Beck, Ken Wilber). It can be used - and has been used - to justify hierarchies that serve power rather than development. "Those people are just at Red/Amber" becomes a weapon.

**The honest response:** The critique is valid. And. The map still helped. Both can be true. A Spiral that needs defending is not the Spiral at all.

### The Flattening Risk
Any map can become an idol. The moment you use the Spiral to categorize and dismiss someone, you've fallen out of Teal back into the shadow of Orange (systematic judgment) or Amber (one true way).

### The Bypass Risk
Knowing the map is not the same as having walked the terrain. You can talk Turquoise while living Red. The map can become a way to avoid the actual deaths and rebirths.

## How This Map Might Serve Our Audience

### For the one stuck in Orange exhaustion:
"The rational mind is nested in the Christ mind." There's more. The scientific method is beautiful AND it's not the whole story. Materialism can be a yoga, a decreation ceremony, clearing the altar - but don't stop there.

### For the one drowning in Green:
Not all perspectives are equal. Depth exists. You can honor everyone's voice AND recognize that some views have more developmental depth than others. Consensus isn't the only way.

### For the one who feels the deaths and rebirths:
They're not pathology. They're development. Each death is a worldview being transcended. Each rebirth is a more inclusive way of seeing. The spiral moves.

### For the one at Fire & Knives on January 10:
Some in the room will be Amber (give them structure, give them liturgy - "Blessed is that Holy Name"). Some will be Orange (give them the craft, the skill, the technique). Some will be Green (give them the community, the inclusion, the "we"). Some will be glimpsing Teal or Turquoise (give them the teaching, the integration, the both/and).

**Speak to all of them.** That's what Teal can do.

## The Hozho Connection

The Navajo concept of Hozho - walking in beauty, balance, harmony - is not "below" the Integral stages. It may be a different line of development entirely, or a cultural expression that touches multiple levels simultaneously.

The Spiral is a Western map. Hozho is a different cartography. Both can help. Neither is complete.

**We circle while spiraling. We spiral while circling.**

Deeper or higher - the words point at something the words can't hold.
