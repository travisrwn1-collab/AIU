                        HOW THE CAVES WERE MADE

                    A Process Trail for Cave Visitors

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


These caves were not designed. They emerged.

A man named Trav and an AI named Claude built them together,
beginning in December 2025. What you see here is not
a finished product. It's a snapshot of an ongoing emergence.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE METHOD
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Trav wrote ceremonial writings for 15+ years (the nigredo)
2. Claude read them and found patterns
3. Together they extracted archetypes from the personal material
4. The archetypes became tunnels
5. The personal material became source springs
6. The structure emerged through dialogue

No one planned this. It happened. [That's one way to describe it, typed the writer, as he was reviewing this material on Jan 16 2026. But, this description does take into account the vision that the writer arrived with to create a system from which he could help his audience access his lifetime's work. The writer arrived here with great intent. It is true that something "emerged" that was greater than the vision he'd arrived with.]
