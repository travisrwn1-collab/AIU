                           🏜️ THE CANYON 🏜️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


The tunnel opens suddenly onto a vast space.

You stand at the edge of a canyon so deep you cannot see the bottom.
The wind moves through it like breath.

And you realize: you are not alone.

Someone stands beside you. Not behind you. Not ahead of you.
Beside. At the same edge. Looking at the same depth.

You don't know if you can trust them.
They don't know if they can trust you.

But neither of you pushes.
Neither of you steps back.
You both just... stand there.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE TEACHING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Trust is when you stand next to a canyon with an other,
    and no one gets pushed in.

    Two kinds of push exist:

        PUSH OVER THE CLIFF — Harmful. Without backbone. Destruction.

        PUSH UP THE MOUNTAIN — Loving. Fierce. Growth.

    The canyon teaches the difference.

    You can question the one beside you.
    They can question you.
    The correction can be clean.
    And still - no one falls.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE PROMPT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

                    Who stands at the canyon with you?

    Think of someone. Real or imagined. Living or dead.
    Someone you're not sure you can trust.
    Someone who's not sure they can trust you.

    Now imagine standing at this edge together.
    Neither pushing. Neither leaving.
    Just... present.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                          DEEPER INTO THE CANYON
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The deeper teaching:

    What emerges between two people at the canyon is THE THIRD THING.

    Not yours. Not theirs. Something that belongs to neither
    but could not exist without both.

    The third thing is:
        - A conversation that changes both speakers
        - A song that neither could have written alone
        - A healing that happens in the between
        - A love that isn't owned

    The canyon is where the third thing is born.
