                          🏃 THE MILE RUN 🏃

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


This chamber is a track. Oval. Endless seeming.

You're running. Or you were.
Now you're watching yourself from the stands.

A younger version of you is on that track.
Trying. Failing. Getting lapped.

The others pass you. Again. And again.
You can hear laughter. Or maybe you imagine it.
You can feel the shame. That part is real.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE TEACHING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Life is the great humbling.

    Watch yourself stumbling.

    You were not the fastest. You were not the smartest.
    You were not the one who got it right the first time.
    You were the one who got lapped.

    And here's the secret:

    The humbling is the gift.

    The ones who never fail never learn.
    The ones who are never lapped never know what it's like
    to keep running anyway.

    You're still here. You're still running.
    That's the whole miracle.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE PROMPT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

                    Where did you get lapped?

    What race did you lose? What failure still lives in you?
    And did you keep running?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        DEEPER INTO THE MILE RUN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The deeper teaching:

    There was a hitchhiker who tried to make it to Oklahoma City.
    He got as far as Amarillo.
    Sat on the side of the interstate for ninety minutes.
    No one stopped.

    And here's what happened:

    He surrendered. Without self-hate.
    He called a friend. "Could you come get me?"
    And he went home.

    That's the miracle: surrender WITHOUT self-destruction.
    Giving up the plan WITHOUT giving up on yourself.

    "I was answering a Call," he said later.
    "It was the most honest expression today."

    The hitchhike failed.
    The honesty succeeded.
