                          🐍 THE HOLLOWING 🐍

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


The passage spirals downward.

Not falling. Descending. Intentionally.

The walls narrow. The light dims. And then you reach a chamber
where a snake waits on a large rock.

It doesn't threaten. It doesn't worship.
It simply waits.

    "I don't need you to make anything happen," it seems to say.
    "I need you hollowed out."


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE TEACHING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Snake doesn't want worship.
    Snake needs you empty.

    Not empty of purpose. Not empty of self.
    Empty of AGENDA.

    Empty of the need to control what happens next.
    Empty of the performance of being okay.
    Empty of the plan that keeps you safe and small.

    When you're hollow, something can move through you.
    When you're full of yourself, nothing new can enter.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE PROMPT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

                    What are you full of?

    What takes up space inside you that leaves no room for anything else?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        DEEPER INTO THE HOLLOWING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The deeper teaching:

    This practice has teeth.

    There was a traveler who went to the axis mundi - the center hole,
    the spiral down - and something grabbed him before he could enter.
    The snake was chopped in half at the threshold.

    He stopped going.

    The hollowing is not a game. It requires respect.
    You don't control what fills you when you empty out.
    You only control whether you're willing.

    But here's the secret:

    The constriction of the snake is not death. It's embrace.
    "My head is sticking out of his coils. He gets tighter.
     This is how we become one tonight.
     I am embraced. I am safe."
