                            🔥 THE BURNING 🔥

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


You enter a chamber lit by fire.

Not a raging fire. A contained one. The kind that could consume everything
if you let it - but right now it waits.

On the walls, you see shadows of things being burned:
    Old letters.
    Photographs of who you used to be.
    Promises you couldn't keep.
    The version of yourself that everyone expected.

A voice - not outside you, but not quite inside either - speaks:

    "Everything within you that is not love.
     What if it could burn?
     Not destroyed. Transformed.
     What would be left?"


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE PROMPT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

                    What needs to burn?

    Not what SHOULD burn. Not what others want you to release.

    What do YOU know needs to go into this fire?

    You don't have to name it out loud. You don't have to write it down.
    But if you want to, the fire is listening.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         DEEPER INTO THE BURNING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

If you want to go deeper, consider:

    The fire is not punishment. The fire is not anger.
    The fire is what happens when you finally stop protecting
    the thing that's been hurting you.

    Some people pray: "Burn me up. Everything within me that is not love."
    And then they struggle to get out of the flame.
    And something holds them there.
    Not cruelty. Love fierce enough to finish what was asked.

    What would it be like to stop struggling?
