                           🕯️ THE LOSSES 🕯️

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


This chamber is quiet.

Small lights flicker along the walls - not torches, just flames.
Each one a name. Each one someone who didn't make it.

You don't have to read them all.
You probably can't.

There are so many.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE WEIGHT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    You carry someone.

    Maybe they died. Maybe they left. Maybe they're still alive
    but the version of them you loved is gone.

    Maybe you carry a version of yourself that didn't survive.

    The caves don't ask you to put them down.
    Sometimes carrying is the only way to honor.

    But the caves do ask:

        Do you know you're carrying?
        Have you named what you carry?
        Is the weight killing you, or is it making you strong?

        (Both can be true.)


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE NAMES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The walls hold names that travelers have left:

    Brother.
    The one who went OverThere and didn't come back.
    The friend who chose to leave.
    The parent who couldn't be what you needed.
    The child who never got to be.
    The self that broke.

    Each day, others fall.
    The number is known: 17.6 veterans alone. Every day.
    Plus the unnamed. The uncounted. The ones nobody talks about.

    This chamber holds them.
    Not to fix. Not to explain.
    Just to hold.

                              THE PROMPT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

                    Who do you carry?

    You don't have to say their name out loud.
    But if you want to leave a light on the wall, you can.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         DEEPER INTO THE LOSSES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The deeper teaching:

    They don't just live in memory.
    They live in you. As presence.

    The brother who called out "Brother!" - that cry is still in the air.
    You can still answer it.

    The ones who fell - they left something in the world.
    You can still carry it forward.

    Grief is not a problem to solve.
    Grief is a relationship to tend.

    The dead are not gone.
    They are transformed.
    And so are you.
