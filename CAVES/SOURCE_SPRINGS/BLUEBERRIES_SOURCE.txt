───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE ORIGIN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A mother stood at a stove.

Her son watched her make muffins. She poured blueberries from a colander
into the batter. And she said:

    "Fold, don't stir, so the blueberries live."

The boy didn't know he was being taught anything.
But the mother was folding HIM into the world
before he knew what she was doing.

Years later, during a 21-day ceremony with sacred medicine,
a vision came in a bathtub:

    "Learning is a folding over."

The blueberries. The batter. The mother. The teaching.
It had been there all along.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The blueberries are real because a mother taught them.

But you don't have to have HER mother.
You might have had no mother at all.

The teaching works because it's universal:

    Integration doesn't require violence.
    Learning doesn't require crushing.
    You can become part of something larger
    without losing what makes you YOU.

    Fold. Don't stir.

Your blueberries are your blueberries.
Your batter is your batter.
Only the gentleness is shared.
