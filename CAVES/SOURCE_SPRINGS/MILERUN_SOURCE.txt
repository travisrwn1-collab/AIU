───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE ORIGIN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A boy ran the mile.

He got lapped.

    "Life is the great humbling. Watch him stumbling."

Years later, a man tried to hitchhike to Oklahoma City.
He got as far as Amarillo. Sat on the side of the interstate
for ninety minutes. No one stopped.

He surrendered. Without self-hate.
Called a friend. "Could you come get me?"
And went home.

    "I was answering a Call," he said later.
    "It was the most honest expression today."

The hitchhike failed.
The honesty succeeded.

The miracle was the transition from determination to surrender
without any hint of self-hate.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The mile run is real because failure is real.

But you don't have to run HIS race.
You have your own.

The teaching works because it's universal:

    You will be humbled.
    You will get lapped.
    You will fail.

    The miracle is not avoiding failure.
    The miracle is surrender without self-destruction.
    Giving up the plan without giving up on yourself.

Your humbling is your humbling.
Your failure is your failure.
Only the getting-back-up is shared.
