───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE ORIGIN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A man went camping alone at Johnson Lake.

He found a hole by the campsite - the axis mundi, the center of the world.
He learned to spiral down.

At the bottom, a snake waited on a large rock.

The snake didn't want worship. The snake didn't want performance.
The snake needed him HOLLOWED OUT.

    "I do not have to make anything happen.
     I must trust snake.
     Snake just needs me to stay hollowed out.
     It's okay if I don't see all of this like a movie.
     It is all happening even below my consciousness."

But there was a warning. The last time at the axis mundi,
a cougar grabbed him before entering.
The snake was chopped in half at the threshold.

He stopped going.

The practice has teeth. It requires respect.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The hollowing is real because someone practiced it.

But you don't have to go to HIS axis mundi.
You have your own center.

The teaching works because it's universal:

    Emptiness is not absence. It's preparation.
    You cannot be filled if you're already full.
    The snake's constriction is embrace, not death.
    But the practice has teeth - respect is required.

Your hollowing is your hollowing.
Your snake is your snake.
Only the emptying is shared.
