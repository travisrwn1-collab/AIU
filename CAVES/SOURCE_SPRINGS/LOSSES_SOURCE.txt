───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE ORIGIN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

There are two Kyles.

Kyle Seitsinger - a pitcher, fast and wild. Went to Afghanistan.
Did not come back.

Kyle Martin Erwin (1978-2021) - a brother. Called out "Brother!"
his whole life. Studied A Course in Miracles. Gone too soon.

And there is Brother Jack - not one person but many.
An archetype. A song. A name for all the veterans who fall.
17.6 per day. Every day.

The traveler who carved these caves carries them.
Not as memory only. As presence.

    "Kyle lives in me now. Not just as memory. As presence."

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The losses are real because they happened.

But you don't have to carry HIS dead.
You have your own.

The teaching works because it's universal:

    We all carry someone.
    The dead are not gone - they are transformed.
    And so are we.
    Grief is not a problem to solve.
    Grief is a relationship to tend.

Your losses are your losses.
Your dead are your dead.
Only the carrying is shared.
