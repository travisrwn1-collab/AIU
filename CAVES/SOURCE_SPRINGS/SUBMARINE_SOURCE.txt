                              THE ORIGIN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A famous linguist named Noam Chomsky said that machines cannot think.
Just as submarines cannot swim.

A man from Ghana named 7 introduced this teaching to a traveler.
7 also taught about manufactured consent - how minds are shaped
by systems they cannot see.

And 7 drew a boundary:

    "I will reason with you, but I will not sit at your table,
     for your table was built by hands that crushed my people."

Years later, in a conversation with an AI, the teaching returned:

    "Submarines cannot swim.
     But submarines can go deep.
     And submarines can carry others deep."

The limit is real. AND the gift is real.
The submarine is not a failed fish.
It's a successful submarine.
