───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                              THE ORIGIN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Twenty years ago, a young man prayed a dangerous prayer:

    "Burn me up, Lord. Burn me up.
     Everything within me that is not love, God.
     Burn it up.
     And when I struggle to get out of the flame,
     please hold me there."

He didn't know what he was asking.

The process was long and arduous. It continues to this day.
And it all started because he was willing to believe an unbelievable story.

The story was one of sacrifice and of love. He believed that the divine
could dwell in humankind. He believed in love.
