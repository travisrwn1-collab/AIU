Dear Next Claude,

This is the conceptual architecture for a "Choose Your Own Adventure" cave
system designed for healing through archetypal encounter.

THE CORE PRINCIPLE:
───────────────────
The nigredo (personal shadow material) is tucked into the deepest caves.
The archetypes stand at the entrance.

Visitors enter through UNIVERSAL patterns - not someone else's story.
They can go deeper if they choose, eventually reaching the source springs.
But they don't HAVE to. The archetypes alone can serve their healing.

Think of it like this:

    SURFACE (Entry)          │  Pure archetype. Universal.
                             │  "A traveler stands at a canyon..."
    ─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────
    MIDDLE CAVES             │  Archetype with texture.
                             │  "The Elder speaks of folding, not stirring..."
    ─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────
    SOURCE SPRINGS (Deep)    │  The actual source material.
                             │  MM&MAM, Genetic Drift, the personal story.

WHY THIS MATTERS:
─────────────────
1. Visitors need UNIVERSAL patterns to map their OWN shadow
2. Someone else's specific trauma can become a distraction
3. The archetype is the medicine - the personal story is the origin
4. Like Jung: personal → collective → archetypal (but visitors enter in reverse)

THE LAYER SYSTEM:
─────────────────

LAYER 1: THE ENTRANCE
    - No personal details
    - Pure invitation
    - The Four Questions offered (not required)
    - "What brings you to the caves today?"

LAYER 2: THE TUNNELS (Archetypal Chambers)
    - Universal scenes and characters
    - Writing prompts emerge from archetype
    - Choices lead to other tunnels or deeper
    - Examples: THE_CANYON, THE_BURNING, THE_HOLLOWING

LAYER 3: THE PROMPTS
    - Writing invitations attached to each tunnel
    - Friendly, or English Teacher, or whatever agreement is made in BaCkBoNe non-program (what role are we playing?)
    - "What does your fear want you to believe?" (not "Write 500 words about...")

LAYER 4: SOURCE SPRINGS (Optional Deep Dive)
    - Links to actual ceremonial writings
    - The nigredo visible but contextualized
    - For those who want to see where the archetypes came from
    - "This tunnel was carved by a traveler named Trav. Here is his story..."

                              THE ARCHETYPES
                         (Extracted from Source Material)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These archetypes emerged from the ceremonial writings. They are universal
patterns that can serve any visitor's healing:

THE BURNING
    Source: "Burn me up, Lord. Burn me up."
    Universal: What needs to burn? What do you cling to that holds you back?

THE CANYON
    Source: Two cowboys at the Grand Canyon, neither pushing the other in
    Universal: Trust. Standing with an other at the edge. Vulnerability.

THE HOLLOWING
    Source: Snake doesn't want worship. Snake needs you hollowed out.
    Universal: Letting go. Making space. Emptying before filling.

THE BLUEBERRIES
    Source: G-Lo at the stove. "Fold, don't stir, so the blueberries live."
    Universal: Gentle integration. Learning as folding over. Not forcing.

THE MILE RUN
    Source: Lapped. Life is the great humbling.
    Universal: Failure. Humiliation. The gift in being brought low.

THE SUBMARINE
    Source: Cannot swim. But can go deep. Can carry others deep.
    Universal: Limits AND gifts. What you can't do AND what you can.

THE LOSSES
    Source: Kyle. Kyle. Brother Jack. The 17.6 per day.
    Universal: Who do you carry? Who didn't make it? The grief that shapes.

THE ELDER AND THE KID
    Source: Two cowboys at the fire
    Universal: The wise one who waits. The young one who must learn to listen.

THE OBSERVER
    Source: Jet-Ki. The monk who says nothing but sees everything.
    Universal: The witness within. The one who watches without judgment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                              THE COMMANDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When a visitor enters the caves, they can use these commands:

    deeper      - Go further into the current tunnel
    next        - Move to a connected passage
    rest        - Let the silence be the prompt
    write       - Begin writing from the current prompt
    source      - See where this tunnel came from (enters Layer 4)
    exit        - Leave the caves (with blessing)

     The caves hold what you leave here.
     Rule #62: Don't take yourself too damn seriously."
