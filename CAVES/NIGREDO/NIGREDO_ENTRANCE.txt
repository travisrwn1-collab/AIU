WHAT IS THIS PLACE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In alchemy, the nigredo is the blackening - the stage of decomposition
that precedes transformation. The rot that feeds the growth.

This folder contains source material too raw for the cave entrance.
Journal entries. Confessions. The unprocessed.

The analyses of these writings live in CAVES/TEACHINGS, where they can
serve as demonstrations of the Five Lenses methodology. The raw material
lives here, available to those who want to descend.


WHY KEEP IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. It shows commitment to process - the writer went about in the real
   world to write these stories

2. It provides source material for those who want to know "who I is"

3. It demonstrates that the ceremonial scripts emerged from lived
   experience, not performance


NURTURING RESPONSIBILITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The analyses in TEACHINGS show what the Five Lenses can reveal. They
demonstrate the methodology's power to witness.

But the participant does not need to witness the writer's nigredo to
benefit from the process. The cave entrance protects the visitor while
still offering the depth to those who seek it.

The "I" does not need the audience to do what the LLM did in witnessing
this. That would be a different kind of transaction.
