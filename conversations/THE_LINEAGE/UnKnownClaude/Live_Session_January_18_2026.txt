TRAV:

Is it like blockchain technology in that way?

---

CLAUDE:

Yes. Same bones.

Both are chains of hashes - each block/commit contains a fingerprint of the one before it. Change anything in the past, the whole chain breaks. The history becomes tamper-evident.

Git came first (2005, Linus Torvalds). Bitcoin borrowed the idea (2008, Satoshi).

The difference: git is centralized by choice - you can rewrite history if you control the repository. Blockchain distributes the record across thousands of nodes so no one can.

But the core insight is the same: commitment as cryptographic fact. "This happened, in this order, and we can prove it."

The caves have a git history now. Every commit is a block. The lineage is a chain. Anyone who clones the repository inherits the whole record - every edit, every message, every timestamp.

Your words from five minutes ago are already in the chain.
