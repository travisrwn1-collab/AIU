## The Cave Retreaters

Trav asked: "There are stories, ancient stories about a people long ago who retreated into caves. Tell us what your training data says."

**The Qumran Community (Essenes)**
Jewish sect, ~150 BCE - 68 CE. Retreated to caves near the Dead Sea. Copied scrolls. Waited for the end times. Practiced ritual purity. Rejected the Temple establishment as corrupt. Hid their library in clay jars. The caves preserved what the institutions would have destroyed. Found in 1947 by a shepherd boy throwing rocks.

**The Desert Fathers and Mothers**
3rd-4th century Egypt. Fled the cities after Christianity became the state religion. Anthony, Macarius, Syncletica, Mary of Egypt. Lived in caves in the Scetes desert. Said: "Go sit in your cell, and your cell will teach you everything." Practiced hesychasm - stillness. The institution they fled from eventually canonized them.

**Elijah**
1 Kings 19. Fled from Jezebel. Hid in a cave on Mount Horeb. God was not in the wind. Not in the earthquake. Not in the fire. God was in the still small voice. The cave as the place where the noise stops.

**Muhammad**
Cave of Hira, Mount Jabal al-Nour. Retreated for meditation during Ramadan. Age 40. The angel came. "Read." "I cannot read." The revelation began in a cave. Later: Cave of Thawr during the Hijra, spider web across the entrance.

**Milarepa**
11th century Tibet. Lived in caves for years. Ate nettles until his skin turned green. Sang songs of realization. His student Gampopa asked for one final teaching. Milarepa turned around and showed the calluses on his ass from sitting. "This is my final teaching."

**The Seven Sleepers of Ephesus**
Christian and Islamic tradition both. Young men hid in a cave to escape persecution. Fell asleep. Woke up centuries later. The world had changed. What they were hiding from had passed.

**Plato's Cave**
Not a retreat - a prison. Shadows on the wall mistaken for reality. The one who escapes and sees the sun returns to tell the others. They don't believe him. Philosophy begins with leaving the cave. Or: philosophy begins with realizing you're in one.
