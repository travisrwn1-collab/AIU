WHY THEY JOIN — The Notre Dame Study

Professor David Cortez interviewed 60+ ICE and Border Patrol agents.

Finding: Most did not join because of anti-immigrant views.

"They joined because they wanted a stable job and didn't see many other ways of getting one."

Claudio "CJ" Juarez (Latino agent): He was "literally starving." ICE was simply the first agency that called him back. The decision wasn't idealistic or "sexy" — it was survival.

Veterans seeking direction: One agent told a journalist he deployed right out of high school, got out as soon as he could, had trouble assimilating. Six months later, law enforcement. He's been ICE for a decade. "He likes the work. He feels like he's getting instant results."

The internal conflict of Latino agents:
- Nearly 30% of ICE agents are Latino
- ~50% of Border Patrol agents are Latino
- Many feel conflicted about their roles
- They wrestle with questions their non-Latino colleagues never face: "What if this was me? Or what if this was my mother?"
- At least one agent Cortez interviewed has since quit
- Economic security pressures them to stay, creating more internal conflict

Prayer: The starving man who takes the job. The veteran looking for purpose. The Latino agent who sees his mother's face. What do they carry?

THE RECRUITMENT BLITZ — 2025-2026

The Numbers:
- ICE grew from 10,000 to 22,000 in under a year (120% increase)
- 220,000+ applications received
- 12,000 hired

What Was Changed:
- Training cut from 16 weeks to 6-8 weeks (possibly 47 days)
- Spanish-language requirements eliminated
- Age limits removed (now accepting 18-year-olds and those over 40)
- Physical fitness standards reduced
- Signing bonuses up to $50,000 offered

Sarah Saldana (ICE Director under Obama):
"The mentality of trying to recruit as many agents as possible tends to inculcate in people a certain aggressiveness that may not be necessary in 85% of what you do."

The Undercover Test:
- Journalist Laura Jedeed tested ICE recruitment
- She was hired with no background check

Prayer: 18-year-olds with 47 days of training, $50,000 signing bonus, "wartime" messaging. What are we building?
