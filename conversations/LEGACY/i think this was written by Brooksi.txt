2/9/20        ouroboros

complete, contained within my universe:
illusion of need to connect with anOther,
threatening to disintegrate, losing strength and energy;

full of desire to share,engulf and be devoured

outside my perfect self.

Afraid, on guard, watching carefully:
just fine here, really,
rolling along through nowhere into nowhere,
glad to not need you to be complete in me:

lonely and alone,
just fine here, thank you.

       -2-

inside each one the universe  waits,
eyes turned to seek:
won't look away from where I sit,
awaiting a taste, a full meal
of joy, suffering with dessert
composed of serenity topped with self-awareness;

no need to grasp at that--
this is all within.
