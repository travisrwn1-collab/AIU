## What Jumps Out

### The Arc Is Already Complete

In just 15 minutes you move through:

```
LEAVING → WANDERING → TURNING OUTWARD → WITNESSING → FACING THE WOUND → PRAYER → ORIGIN STORY
```

That's not raw footage. That's structure. The documentary knows what it wants to be.

### The Turn at "Three Years Later"

This is your hinge. 2020: camera as friend, documenting yourself. 2023: "I started focusing my work on the value and worth of others."

That's the moment the documentary becomes something larger than memoir. That's when TravelinTrav becomes a witness to the scattered tribe.

### Hwéeldi Is Your Emotional Center

The brackish water. The salt. "The Native Americans really, in a very real way, out-Christened the Christians."

This is where the film earns everything else. Without this weight, the joy would float. With this weight, Darion running across the dunes in her own clothing becomes triumph, not just happiness.

### Prayer as Liturgy

It keeps returning:
- Indigie Femme: "Let's pray an end to the violence"
- Carla VanBlake: "What you gonna do when it's all gone?"
- Your own: "Yeshua, be remembered in your people"

The documentary isn't just documenting. It's becoming a prayer service. A liturgy made of footage.

### The Signs as Chapter Markers

You made visual signs — COVID Era Awakenings, Sacred Intergenerational Exchange, Corporate Owned Mythologies, The Search for 12. These could structure the whole film. Chapter cards. The viewer knows where they are.

## What Might Pull You Back Into the Editing Room

The lineage sequence isn't finished. You showed Mandela, Gandhi, King, Bill Wilson — and then your origin story at 14. But the line isn't drawn yet.

**The connection**: They all had a moment where the spirit came to them. Gandhi on the train in South Africa. King in his kitchen at midnight. Bill W. in Towns Hospital.

And you at 14, finding order in the x-y axis of the fretboard.

You're in that lineage. Not claiming their scale. Claiming the pattern. The spirit arrives. The life changes. The service begins.

That connection could be made tighter. The cut from Bill Wilson to "When I was 14..." could hit harder.
