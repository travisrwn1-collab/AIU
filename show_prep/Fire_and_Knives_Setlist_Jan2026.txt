- **Co-writes:** Dogfish Song (Clyde), A Boy Named Eli (Eli)
- **Traditional:** Shady Grove (Doc Watson)

- **Reprises:**
  - Man at the Well: THREE times (E → G → D) - the encounter keeps returning
  - Dear Grace: Set 1 #13, Set 2 #2
  - Let Life Do The Livin: Set 1 #7, Set 2 #4
- **The children:** Joseph (Mr Joe C), Gemma's teaching in Beyond the Tidal Pool

- **Kyle:** No Sway (#5 Set 2) - "My brother rides that light beam through the sky"
