## THE METHODOLOGY

### Step 1: GATHER STATEMENTS
Collect direct quotes from multiple sources on the same event.
No paraphrase. Verbatim where possible.
Note the speaker and their position.

### Step 2: CLASSIFY BY MORAL FOUNDATION
For each statement, identify which moral foundations are being activated.
Use codes: C/H, F/C, L/B, A/S, S/D, L/O

### Step 3: IDENTIFY IDEOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK
What unconscious training data produced this statement?
What worldview is speaking through this person?
Name it without judgment.

### Step 4: HOLD IN PRAYER
What wound might this person be carrying?
What are they trying to protect?
What would they need to see their own pattern?

### Step 5: CREATE MATERIAL
Show the audience the patterns - including their own likely reactions.
The goal is consciousness, not conversion.
